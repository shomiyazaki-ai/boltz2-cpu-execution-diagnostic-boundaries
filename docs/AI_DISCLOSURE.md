# AI assistance and responsibility

AI assistance was used during the historical study for planning, code/harness support and interpretation, and in this publication-preparation Work for evidence organization, English drafting, figure design, curation, verification-code generation and QC support. Exact model identifiers and a complete historical prompt ledger were not supplied and are not invented.

The personal Windows CPU attempts and small-worker tests are historical author-run observations. The current Work reads their preserved files, recomputes sample/table arithmetic, checks event/state consistency, renders two figures, statically examines code and compares packages. It does not rerun Windows dynamic tests, Boltz inference, tensor comparisons or a multi-GiB memory stress test.

Production and later QC use separate scripts/comparison paths in the same Work. This provides internal pre-release checking, not independent external peer review. AI is not an author, does not supply a safety certification, and cannot establish historical facts beyond the evidence provided. Sho Miyazaki is responsible for scientific interpretation, rights, final content and publication approval.
